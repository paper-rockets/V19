# Fable Cities — 3D Procedural Terrain & Buildings Engine

A standalone procedural 3D map generator featuring:
- **Procedural Terrain Engine**: Dynamic heightmaps, coastal shorelines, mountains, rivers, and PBR terrain shaders.
- **Procedural City & Buildings**: Hundreds of stepped skyscrapers, Art Deco towers, neoclassical dome hall, and conforming road networks.
- **Mobile Touch Controls**: Full mobile support with 2-finger pinch zoom, 2-finger pan, rotate, tilt, and an on-screen touch HUD.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Procedural sky, dynamic sunlight, shadows, and day/night shaders.

## Controls

### Mobile Phone / Touchscreen
- **2-Finger Pinch**: Zoom in and zoom out smoothly.
- **2-Finger Drag**: Pan across the landscape.
- **2-Finger Twist**: Rotate camera view.
- **1-Finger Drag**: Orbit and tilt around the city.
- **Touch HUD**: On-screen buttons for Zoom (`+` / `-`), Directional Pan (D-pad), Rotation, Tilt, and instant Camera View Presets (City, Top-Down, Street, Aerial, Skyline).

### Desktop / Mouse
- **Left Mouse Button + Drag**: Orbit and tilt.
- **Right Mouse Button + Drag**: Pan camera.
- **Mouse Wheel**: Zoom in and zoom out.
