How Each Control Operates

  • Top Triangle (▲): Completely replaces the top ball. Pulling it moves the layer straight UP.
  • Bottom Triangle (▼): Completely replaces the bottom ball. Pulling it moves the layer straight DOWN.
  • -X Sphere & its opposite side: Pulling either sphere moves the layer LEFT / RIGHT.
  • Z Sphere & its opposite side: Pulling either sphere moves the layer FORWARD / BACKWARD in depth.
  • Outer Curved Brackets ( ): Dragging around either bracket rotates the layer flat like a dial.
  • Center 3D Space: Tumbles the gizmo in 3D so you can view all axes from any angle.
